#**Main learning path babak 1, lainnya adalah second option**
## **BABAK 1: FONDASI A1 — KOSAKATA & KALIMAT DASAR**
*Target: Pengguna bisa memperkenalkan diri, menyebut angka, warna, dan benda sehari-hari.*

**Unit 1: Salam & Perkenalan** *(seperti gambar)*
- Level 1: Salam formal/informal (Hello, Hi, Good morning, Good night)
- Level 2: Nama & asal (My name is..., I am from...)
- Level 3: Subject pronouns (I, You, He, She, It, We, They)
- Level 4: To be (am/is/are) — positif, negatif, tanya
- Level 5: Listening: percakapan perkenalan
- Level 6: Speaking: memperkenalkan diri

**Unit 2: Angka & Waktu**
- Level 1: Angka 1–100
- Level 2: Jam (What time is it? It's...)
- Level 3: Hari, bulan, tahun
- Level 4: Preposisi waktu (in, on, at)
- Level 5: Listening: jadwal harian
- Level 6: Speaking: menyebutkan waktu

**Unit 3: Warna & Bentuk**
- Level 1: Warna dasar & lanjutan
- Level 2: Bentuk (circle, square, triangle)
- Level 3: Adjectives (big, small, long, short)
- Level 4: Demonstrative (this/that/these/those)
- Level 5: Listening: deskripsi benda
- Level 6: Speaking: mendeskripsikan benda di sekitar

**Unit 4: Keluarga & Teman**
- Level 1: Anggota keluarga (mother, father, sister, brother, cousin)
- Level 2: Possessive adjectives (my, your, his, her, our, their)
- Level 3: Possessive 's (Sarah's book)
- Level 4: Have/has (I have a sister)
- Level 5: Listening: silsilah keluarga
- Level 6: Speaking: mendeskripsikan keluarga

**Unit 5: Makanan & Minuman**
- Level 1: Kosakata makanan & minuman
- Level 2: Countable vs uncountable (apple vs water)
- Level 3: Article (a/an/the)
- Level 4: Some/any (I have some apples)
- Level 5: Listening: memesan makanan
- Level 6: Speaking: menyatakan suka/tidak suka (I like/I don't like)

**Unit 6: Tubuh & Kesehatan Dasar**
- Level 1: Bagian tubuh (head, hand, leg, eyes)
- Level 2: Have/has + body parts (I have two eyes)
- Level 3: Sakit sederhana (I have a headache)
- Level 4: Imperative (Open your mouth, Touch your nose)
- Level 5: Listening: instruksi dokter sederhana
- Level 6: Speaking: menyatakan kondisi tubuh

**Unit 7: Rumah & Perabotan**
- Level 1: Ruangan (kitchen, bedroom, bathroom)
- Level 2: Perabotan (table, chair, bed, sofa)
- Level 3: Preposisi tempat (in, on, under, behind, next to)
- Level 4: There is/There are
- Level 5: Listening: deskripsi rumah
- Level 6: Speaking: mendeskripsikan rumah sendiri

**Unit 8: Pakaian & Warna Lanjutan**
- Level 1: Jenis pakaian (shirt, pants, shoes, jacket)
- Level 2: Warna & pola (striped, polka dot, plain)
- Level 3: Adjective order (a beautiful red dress)
- Level 4: This/that/these/those + clothes
- Level 5: Listening: belanja pakaian
- Level 6: Speaking: mendeskripsikan pakaian yang dipakai

**Ujian Babak 1:** Tes komprehensif A1 (listening, reading, grammar, speaking).

**Unit 23: Peristiwa Bersejarah Sederhana**
